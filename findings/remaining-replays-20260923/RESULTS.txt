E2: CHEAP CONTEXTUAL ONLINE ROUTER
Beta-Bernoulli worker estimates are conditioned on question hop count. The
development prior counts each task once using its mean over two decodes.
Epsilon is selected from 0, .05, .1, .2, .4 by leave-one-stream-out development
evaluation; .05 wins. During test, only earlier production and triggered audit
labels update the router. Full-candidate outcomes are evaluator-only.
This is a stronger contextual baseline than the old unconditioned exploration
arm, but is not a state-of-the-art router reproduction.

With no extra audits: success 65.333%, cost $0.009624468/task, net $0.153708865.
At audit rate .005: same success, cost $0.009688668, net $0.153644665.
At .02 and .1, success remains unchanged while audit costs increase.
The primary premium/static comparator previously achieved 66% and net
$0.155095067. These results do not establish a benefit from auditing here.

E6: MATCHED AUDITS AND POOLING
The pooling ablations use the SAME nonuniform audit law [1,2,4,8], randomness,
audit labels and costs. Rates: .005, .02, .1; budget $.20; reward $.25.
Arms: equal, exponential, generalized Bayesian, online convex stacking,
unweighted updates, three-step-delayed updates, and each single forecaster.
Unlike the old no-audit equal arm, equal-same-audits pays identical audit costs.
The contextual router uses uniform audits and is not a spending-matched control
for these nonuniform pooling arms.

At .005, all pooling and single-forecaster arms attain 66% success.
Equal-same-audits and exponential both cost $0.020946171, net $0.144053829.
The single mini forecaster costs $0.011192091, net $0.153807909, at the same
success rate. In this frozen-forecast setting, extra forecasters add overhead
without an observed routing gain. This does not test adaptive semantic reports.
All 31 routing arms have zero total-budget violations.
Complete results: routing-summary.csv and routing-traces.jsonl.

E7: PROPENSITY ESTIMATION ON REAL MATRICES
Target: average quadratic score across all four candidates and all 150 tasks,
conditional on retained reports and on the mean of the two observed decodes.
We vary uniform/nonuniform auditing, three rates, and unclipped/10/50/100 weights.
Each setting has 1,000 audit-only Monte Carlo draws; exact conditional moments
are computed independently. No new outcome draws or model calls are simulated.

Unclipped inverse-propensity estimates have exact zero conditional bias.
But low audit rates produce substantial variance: for forecaster 0, nonuniform
audits, rate .005, exact RMSE is .540506, mean effective sample size only 2.677,
and maximum inverse probability 903.127. At clip 100, bias is -.361832 and
RMSE .419722. Clipping changes the estimand in expectation; it is not free.
The score average is normalized to [0,1], although unbiased sparse estimates
can exceed 1. Quantiles are normalized score estimates, NOT dollars paid.
Forecaster-rank recovery is descriptive; Monte Carlo trials are not new tasks.
These results concern estimator noise, not causal policy-value improvement.
Complete results: propensity-summary.csv and propensity-monte-carlo.jsonl.

E3: COALITION AFFINE REPORT ATTACKS (SEPARATE DIRECTORY)
calms_runs/coalition-attacks-20260923 contains 120 fitted settings and 18,000
