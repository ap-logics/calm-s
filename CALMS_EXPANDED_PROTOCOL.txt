CALM-S EXPANDED STUDY -- 23 SEPTEMBER 2026

Authorization: user permits reasonable experiment spending below US$100.
Execution ceiling: US$99 cumulative in the existing ledger, including the
US$2.6840378 first pilot. No change to API keys or the unrelated aamas folder.

Why this run:
The first development data showed overly optimistic cheap-worker forecasts,
and routing subtracted worst-case cost reserves from expected reward. The new
run distinguishes expected cost for utility from conservative affordability
reserves. The original objective remains an explicit offline ablation.
Capability cards contain aggregate DEVELOPMENT performance only.

New data:
60 whole-question QA development tasks, 150 fresh test tasks, each with four
workers x two decodes plus three pre-outcome forecasters.
12 further development projects yield 36 node calibration tasks.
24 separate decomposition-derived projects run on-policy under static,
exploration, equal-pool, selected-only, and CALM-S policies. Agents receive
actual upstream outputs. All component question IDs are disjoint across new
groups and both groups of the earlier pilot.

Models: GPT-4.1 mini, Claude Haiku 4.5, GPT-4.1, Claude Sonnet 5 workers;
the first three also act in separate forecasting contexts.

QA protocol:
Ten disjoint streams of 15 test tasks; pool reset between streams.
Primary budget US$0.20; US$0.10 and US$0.40 sensitivity.
Audit rates 0, 0.5%, 1%, 2%, 5%, 10%; select positive rate on new development
data only. Choose strongest baseline on development, always show equal pooling.
Reward US$0.25 equivalent; report predeclared US$0.05, 0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 1.00
sensitivity rather than changing conversion to obtain a favorable result.
Inference uses paired disjoint stream means, not cached-repeat pseudo-seeds.

Workflow protocol:
Three disjoint streams of eight projects; five policies; budget US$0.60 per
project, audit rate 2%. Fail fast at an incorrect node. Historical static and
cost priors fitted from development nodes with known upstream answers.
Descriptive evidence only at three streams. This is not AppWorld.

Additional offline diagnostics fixed during development collection, BEFORE
new test collection or outcome inspection:
- Cheap hop-conditioned historical predictor fitted on development only.
- Full-candidate forecast Brier against cheap historical predictors.
- Count-matched feedback: same fixed equal-pool selector and same audit trigger
  across arms, reveal one random-worker or one selected-worker outcome per
  triggered task. Update only afterward. Compare next-task full-candidate Brier;
  this isolates a label-selection diagnostic, not a deployment utility claim.
  Reuse the dev-selected audit rate and also show 10% as a fixed sensitivity.
- Report costs, audit counts, abstentions, incomplete responses, and hop strata.

This is a prospective extension after an inspected pilot, not external
preregistration. MuSiQue public-data contamination and exact-match limitations
remain. No actual economic transfers. Coding/EvalPlus, AppWorld, faithful
Agora/AgentLance reproductions, and stronger contextual online learners remain
outside this run. Keep all unfavorable results.
